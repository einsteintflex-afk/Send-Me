Send Me

Campus essentials from the shop opposite R Block, delivered to your hostel or a campus drop-off point. The phone below is a working prototype. Tap through it from sign-in to order tracking.

Prices are not filled in. The product list you mentioned did not arrive, so every item shows "Price to come" and no total is calculated. Product names come from your brief. To load real prices, add them to the PRICES object near the end of the script (for example "coke-1l": 12.5) and cart, subtotal and total update by themselves. Shop phone numbers, delivery fee and rider details are placeholders for the same reason.

Design concept

A friendly shopfront for hostel life. Green for trust and campus, gold for the things you act on. Big tap targets, one-handed reach, and plain words like hostel, block and room number.

Primary flow

  • Sign up with name, phone, email, hostel, room
  • Home, then shop or search
  • Product, then cart
  • Checkout and pay in app
  • Track: Pending to Delivered

Interactions

  • Search filters the catalog as you type
  • Category chips narrow the list, tap again to clear
  • Quantity stepper on cards, details and cart
  • Minus at 1 removes the item
  • "Can't find it?" is on home, shop, empty search, cart
  • Cart tab shows a live item count

Layout and labels

  • Two-column product grid, 16px gutters
  • Bottom tabs: Home, Shop, Cart, Orders
  • Statuses used exactly: Pending, Accepted, Shopping, On the Way, Delivered, Cancelled
  • Availability reads "Listed as available". No stock counts shown.
  • Cancel is offered only while Pending

Open items

  • Product prices and any quantities
  • UGMSA Mini Mart phone numbers
  • Delivery fee rule
  • Rider name and contact (screen is ready for them)
  • Real product photos
